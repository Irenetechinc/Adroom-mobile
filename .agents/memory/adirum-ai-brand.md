---
name: Adirum AI customer-facing brand
description: Brand naming guidance for public pages and provider-return screens in this workspace.
---

Customer-facing branding uses “Adirum AI.” OAuth success, error, and cancellation pages, account pages, and user-facing notifications should display this name. Legacy `adroom://` callback schemes, package identifiers, and deployed domains are operational identifiers and should remain unchanged unless a coordinated migration is requested.

**Why:** The user identified Adirum AI as the new brand and requested that OAuth redirect pages use it.

**How to apply:** Update visible copy, titles, and notification text, but do not rename callback schemes or production domains as part of a visual rebrand.
