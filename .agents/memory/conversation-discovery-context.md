---
name: Conversation discovery context
description: Discovery must be anchored to the strategy's actual product, brand, or service identity and demand language.
---

Conversation discovery must resolve the promoted offer from the strategy's product data, then search for public demand signals such as requests, recommendations, prices, availability, and buying questions. A strategy title is campaign metadata, not product identity.

**Why:** Title-based searches produce generic campaign-related results and can create engagement work unrelated to what the user is promoting.

**How to apply:** Keep product lookup and search-query construction separate from strategy naming. If no real product, brand, category, or service context exists, skip discovery explicitly instead of substituting the title.
